Refund Policy

Effective Date: 15th Feb, 2025

1. No Refunds on Registration Fees

Once a player completes the registration and payment process for Pro HPCL, the registration fee is non-refundable under any circumstances,

2. Eligibility Criteria & Player Participation

  • Only 1 player from outside Himachal is allowed in Pro HPCL Season 4, a gesture to promote local talent.
  • If a player is found to have provided false information during registration, their participation will be canceled without a refund.

3. Payment Errors & Duplicate Transactions

Refunds will only be considered in cases of:

  • Duplicate payments due to technical errors (subject to verification by our payment gateway).
  • Transaction failures where the amount has been deducted but the registration is not confirmed.

In such cases, players must submit a refund request within 7 days of the transaction by emailing contact@prohpcl.com with proof of payment. After verification, eligible refunds (if any) will be processed within 10-15 working days.

4. Changes & Cancellations by Pro HPCL

  • If Pro HPCL makes any significant changes or cancellations to the event, alternative options such as rescheduling or credit towards a future season may be provided.
  • In case of league cancellation due to government regulations, pandemics, or unavoidable circumstances, Pro HPCL will determine the next course of action at its sole discretion.
