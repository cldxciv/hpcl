Why Become a Pro HPCL Member?

Every young cricketer deserves an opportunity to showcase their talent. Across Himachal Pradesh, many players practise with dedication but struggle to find the right platform, recognition and financial opportunities. Pro HPCL is working to change that by promoting a more professional cricket culture and creating opportunities for local talent.

More than cricket: building a better future

Through its franchise-based structure and player-auction model, the league promotes a culture in which players can receive financial recognition for their abilities, gain exposure and work towards earning through cricket.

Your membership supports a bigger mission

Becoming a member means joining a community that believes local talent deserves opportunities. Membership revenue can help support the league's wider activities and development initiatives, subject to the programme's operating costs and budget.

Promoting fitness and a drug-free Himachal

One of Pro HPCL's key social objectives is to encourage young people to stay away from drugs and embrace healthier lifestyles — to practise consistently, focus on fitness, build discipline and work towards long-term goals. Say NO to drugs. Say YES to sports, fitness and a brighter future.

Grow your business with Pro HPCL

Membership offers practical benefits for business owners and entrepreneurs: a dedicated business profile on the official Pro HPCL website, and — at higher tiers — PR videos, podcast interviews, promotional reels, livestream opportunities and recognition at selected events.
