About us

Pro HPCL is the ultimate cricketing extravaganza in Himachal Pradesh, dedicated to providing a professional platform for local talent. With its focus on authenticity and excellence, Pro HPCL stands out as one of the few leagues in the region to play with a white leather ball.

We are Welfare Sports Association, as an organization, wish to provide a good cricketing platform wherein our local youth can participate and showcase their talent and move a step forward and play professional cricket and bring laurels to the State.

History & Timeline

Pro Himachal Premium Cricket League (Pro HPCL) is Himachal Pradesh's premier franchise-based T20 tournament. Built as a digital-first white-leather-ball league with an IPL-style format, it has evolved rapidly and is expanding season by season with growing participation and performance metrics.

Timeline of Development

  • April 24, 2022: Pro HPCL was officially inaugurated in Bharari, Shimla, by Urban Development Minister Suresh Bhardwaj.
  • Season 1 (2022): Winner: Jindal Warriors • Runner-up: Rising Stars Baldeha • Winner Prize: ₹5.55 Lakh.
  • Season 2 (2023): Winner: Yorkshire Mandyal • Runner-up: Mandi Marvels • Winner Prize: ₹7 Lakh. Yorkshire Mandyal secured a historic final win by defending 113 runs (April 28, 2023).
  • Season 3 (2025): Winner: Wine Association & Cyberbells Tech Titans (Joint) • Runner-up: N/A • Winner Prize: ₹9 Lakh. The season featured 20 teams, 47 matches, and 12,000+ runs.
  • Season 4 (2026): Winner: TBD • Runner-up: TBD • Winner Prize: ₹14 Lakh. Trials and registrations opened in January 2026 with stronger competition and new franchise entries.
  • December 2025: Founders Veenu Diwan, Abhay Thakur (Lucky), and Ajay Thakur (Kranti) launched Shimla's first full-sized professional cricket ground near Mehli.
  • February 21, 2026: Season registrations remained active as the league prepared for a bigger and better return.

Current Infrastructure: The Mehli Ground

A major milestone was achieved in December 2025 with the completion of a private, full-sized professional cricket arena in Padhechi village (near Mehli, Shimla).

  • Dimensions: 120 x 91 meters field.
  • Features: American turf grass and state-of-the-art night lighting for day-night matches.
  • Goal: To become Pro HPCL's permanent home and eventually host a cricket academy.
