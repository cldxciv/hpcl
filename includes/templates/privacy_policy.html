Privacy Policy

Pro Himachal Premium Cricket League (Pro HPCL) respects the privacy of its players, users, and visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website www.prohpcl.com or register for the league.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or register for Pro HPCL, we may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information: Name, date of birth, contact details (phone number, email), address, Aadhaar number, and other identity-related information. Payment Information: When you register for the league, we collect payment-related details for transaction purposes.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with our website, including IP address, device type, and browsing history.

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To process and confirm your registration for Pro HPCL.
  • To verify your eligibility as a Himachal Pradesh resident for participation in the league.
  • To communicate important updates about matches, trials, auctions, and league events.
  • To process payments and provide receipts.
  • To improve our website, services, and user experience.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. However, we may share your information with:

  • Franchise Owners: Your profile and performance data may be shared with franchise owners for selection and team allocation.
  • Service Providers: Third-party payment processors or IT support teams who help us operate the website and manage transactions.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law or to protect the integrity of the league, we may disclose information to regulatory bodies or law enforcement agencies.

4. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. However, no method of data transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at contact@prohpcl.com. You may opt-out of promotional emails or messages at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions provided. Please note that certain data (such as registration details) cannot be deleted once submitted for participation in the league.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and tracking technologies to enhance user experience and analyze website traffic. You can control cookie settings through your browser.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Pro HPCL reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and continued use of our website after changes are made constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
